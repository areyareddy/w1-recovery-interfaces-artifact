2. The expression `(n + 1000)/70` is a real number, but since `n` is a natural number, `(n + 1000)/70` must be an integer for the equality to hold with `floor(√n)` (which is an integer). Therefore, `(n + 1000)/70` must be an integer, i.e., `n + 1000 ≡ 0 mod 70`, or equivalently `n ≡ 30 mod 70`.
