### Step 1: Prove `f(1) = 0`

Set `x = y = 1` in the functional equation:
`f(1 * 1) = f(1) + f(1)` ⇒ `f(1) = 2 f(1)` ⇒ `f(1) = 0`.

We have `f(1) = f(p * (1/p)) = f(p) + f(1/p)`. Since `f(1) = 0` and `f(p) = p`, we get `0 = p + f(1/p)` ⇒ `f(1/p) = -p`.

Therefore, `f(25/11) = 10 + (-11) = -1 < 0`.
